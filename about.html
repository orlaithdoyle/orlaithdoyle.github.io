About me

OD

I studied physics with astronomy at DCU before doing an MSc in Space Science and Technology at UCD. Remote sensing grabbed me during the MSc, especially radar, because it sees through cloud and darkness and still has a lot left to discover.

At ESA ESTEC I worked on BIOMASS, ESA's first P-band radar satellite. It was built to measure forests, and my job was to see what it could tell us about the ocean. Most days involved writing Python, reading papers, and chasing down why a number looked wrong. Sometimes the answer was physics and sometimes it was a file format.

I like work where the science and the engineering meet, and I like explaining it. I've tutored physics and maths for years, coached kids at my local GAA club, and helped school teams build satellites the size of a soft drink can.

I'm now looking for roles in Earth observation, geospatial analysis or the wider space sector, in Ireland or elsewhere in Europe.
