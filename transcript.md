# Transcript

The modules I studied and what each one covered. Official transcripts from UCD and DCU are available on request.

## MSc Space Science and Technology

*University College Dublin, 2025 to 2026. First Class Honours.*

| Module | What it covered |
|---|---|
| Space Sector Internship | Six-month placement at ESA ESTEC on the Biomass mission, ending in a full research report and poster. |
| Satellite Subsystems | Designing, building and flying a small balloon payload (a TupperSat) through formal design reviews written to ECSS standards. |
| Spacecraft Operations | Running a satellite as part of an operations team: pass planning, commanding, telemetry and handling anomalies, tested on the EIRSAT-1 FlatSat. |
| Space Mission Design Skills | Taking a mission from science goal to requirements, payload and spacecraft design, as a group mission study. |
| Space Detector Laboratory | Hands-on work with radiation detectors used in space, including calibration and performance testing. |
| SAR and InSAR Remote Sensing | Radar imaging, interferometry and polarimetry, applied to real Sentinel-1 and other satellite data. |
| Applications of Space Science | How satellite data is used in practice, across Earth observation, navigation and communications. |
| The Space Environment | Radiation, plasma, the Sun's influence and orbits, and how they affect spacecraft. |
| Planetary Geomorphology | How surfaces on planets and moons are shaped by water, ice, wind and impacts, using spacecraft data. |
| Space Sector Professional Skills | How the European space sector works, from agencies and industry to project management and careers. |
| Technical Communications | Writing and presenting technical work clearly for different audiences. |
| Applied Geophysics | Geophysical survey methods such as gravity, magnetics and seismic (taken as an audit). |
